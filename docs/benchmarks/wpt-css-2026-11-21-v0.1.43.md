Newly passing: `content-visibility-049`, `-055`, `-076` (css-contain) and
`aspect-ratio/replaced-element-003` (css-sizing).

Two tests that passed with 0.1.42 fail. Both passed only because a video we
cannot decode "played" without a picture: `mix-blend-mode-video` (an H.264
source, unsupported on Linux, previously painted as a black box with a play
button) and `corner-shape-backdrop-filter-video-overflow` (no decodable
source; it waited on a `canplaythrough` that the fake playback used to fire).
Undecodable video-only files now fail as unsupported, so pages fall back to
their next `<source>`, and a video without a poster, frame or controls is
transparent, as in browsers.

On this source the full test262 suite showed no regression against
`crates/js/tests/test262_baseline.txt`, with default JIT settings and with
the JIT forced on every function.
