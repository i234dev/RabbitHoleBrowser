pinned manifest (WPT 831baf28; the 1,946 wptserve variants were not run) on
27 November 2026 (Europe/Madrid) on TheOtherSide (Intel i7-12700H, WSL2 Linux
x86-64), 0.2.50 integration `ea1b94b4`, profile ci, `rh-wpt -j 8 --timeout
5000`, isolated loopback HTTP, CPUs 0-7, other build lanes quiet.

**1,943,677 / 2,118,802 subtests (91.7 %)**, against 1,578,590 / 1,757,131
(89.8 %) for 0.2.48 on the same pages. Paired by URL (ports normalized):

## Video and memory (Windows)

Measured by the video work on 26-27 November 2026 on the same laptop under
Windows 11 (Intel i7-12700H), native GUI, isolated profiles, audio output
disabled, public video page with the consent dialog answered:

- Video frames are presented by the user interface independently of the page
  thread: a page blocked by script for 1.5 s kept presenting frames (0 before,
  30 at 30 fps after); the public video page went from 5.4–10.4 fps to
  20.3–22.4 fps in 30-second windows (four alternating runs).
- Opus audio is decoded by libopus 1.6.1 (statically linked); the playback
  clock runs in real time (it advanced at a quarter of real time before).
- Fonts build glyph geometry only for glyphs that are rasterized: resident
  memory attributed to fonts on the video page went from 237 to 44 MiB; a page
  with 850 paragraphs in 8 font families from 1,458 to 249 ms and from 722 to
  111 MiB peak working set, with identical screenshots.
- Mutation and performance observers no longer stay alive after being
  disconnected and unreachable.
