**16,296 / 19,627 (83.0 %)**; 0.1.39 had 16,239.

`line-height: normal` now comes from the first available font's metrics
(rounded ascent + descent + line gap, OS/2 typographic metrics when
USE_TYPO_METRICS is set, otherwise hhea), and the `ascent-override`,
`descent-override` and `line-gap-override` descriptors are supported.
Newly passing include CSS2 `font-011`…`016`, `029`…`032`, `043`, visudet
`line-height-205`/`206`, the metric-override and first-available-font tests,
and tests in css-backgrounds, css-transforms, css-position and css-text.

Five tests that passed with 0.1.39 fail:
- `margin-em-inherit-001` and `padding-em-inherit-001` (CSS2): their
  references assume Times-like metrics (a 47 px normal line at 40 px); the
  bundled DejaVu Serif gives 46 px. They passed with the former 1.2 em rule.
- `clip-text-on-body-not-propagated-to-root` (css-backgrounds) and
  `text-overflow-ellipsis-002` (css-overflow): Ahem lines are now exactly
  one em high with a fractional ascent; glyphs drawn at the rounded baseline
  overhang their box by 0.2 px and the clip cuts that row. A rasterizer
  matter (hinting to the pixel grid), not line layout.
- `flexbox-align-self-vert-001` (css-flexbox): two pixels of one word's
  anti-aliasing differ at a fractional text position.
